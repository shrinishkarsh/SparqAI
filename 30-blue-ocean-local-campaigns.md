# SparqLabs — 30 Blue-Ocean Campaigns (Local & Under-Saturated)

**Thesis:** Cold-outbound saturation is concentrated where tech sells to tech — SaaS,
agencies, fintech buyers are hammered daily. The opportunity is the opposite end:
**local and regional B2B**, where:

1. **Inboxes are nearly untouched** — a commercial HVAC owner or a regional
   distributor gets ~0 cold emails a week. Reply rates run multiples higher.
2. **TAM is enormous and fragmented** — tens of thousands of near-identical
   businesses per niche = the *same* campaign runs across thousands of accounts =
   real **numbers**, and a repeatable, productizable play.
3. **The offer is easy** — one booked commercial account or recurring contract is
   worth thousands, so "we book you qualified meetings" is an instant yes.

**Framing:** SparqLabs sells outbound/appointment-setting to these businesses *and*
runs the outbound on their behalf to **their** customers (property managers, GCs,
local employers, referral sources, procurement). Both sides have clean inboxes.

**Legend:** `ICP` = who SparqLabs sells to · `Books them` = who we book *for* them ·
**Sat** = saturation (lower = better) · **TAM** = volume read.

**Variable key:** `{{first_name}} {{company}} {{location_city}} {{niche}}
{{trigger_detail}} {{similar_company}} {{proof_metric}} {{service_line}} {{role}}`

## Why these "do numbers" (and the launch shortlist)

- **Saturation:** ~24 of 30 are **Very Low** — these buyers essentially never receive
  a relevant cold email, so reply/positive rates run multiples above tech outbound.
- **Volume:** every niche has **tens of thousands** of near-identical businesses, so a
  *single* campaign (one list build, one message system) scales across thousands of
  accounts — the definition of "doing numbers."
- **Productizable:** within a cluster the pain/offer/copy barely changes between
  cities, so you template once and roll city-to-city (a clean **location→location** play).
